# AI System Design

A personal learning repository documenting my study of **AI system
design**, with a focus on understanding how to design reliable,
scalable, cost-aware, and production-ready AI applications.

The repository follows a structured set of AI system design questions,
progressing from foundational concepts to intermediate design problems
and advanced production scenarios. I'll use it to collect my handwritten
notes, diagrams, design reasoning, and other study material as I work
through the topics.

## What I'm Studying

### Foundations

Core concepts behind production LLM applications, including:

-   AI system design interview structure and non-functional requirements
-   Estimation, latency, throughput, and processing patterns
-   Caching, LLM gateways, model routing, rate limits, and quotas
-   Guardrails, prompt management, feedback loops, and AI product
    metrics
-   Choosing between classical machine learning and LLM-based approaches
-   Handling nondeterminism and failures

### Intermediate Design and Debugging

Applying those concepts to practical AI products and operational
problems, such as:

-   Semantic caching and structured document extraction
-   Content moderation, customer-support suggestions, and meeting
    summarization
-   Text-to-SQL and semantic product search
-   A/B testing, token streaming, conversation storage, and memory
-   Privacy, data residency, and quality drift
-   Provider outages, traffic spikes, rising inference costs, and batch
    processing
-   Voice agents, writing assistants, and recommendation systems

### Production-Scale System Design

Working through larger systems and more demanding design constraints,
including:

-   Consumer chat applications and code-completion systems
-   AI tutoring platforms and enterprise AI platforms
-   ML-assisted fraud investigation and customer-feedback analytics
-   Fine-tuning platforms and localization pipelines
-   Personalized notifications and insurance-claims processing
-   Latency targets, build-versus-buy decisions, AI safety,
    observability, and end-to-end system design interviews

## What This Repository Contains

As I study, this repository will contain materials such as:

-   Handwritten notes and their supporting files
-   System architecture diagrams and design sketches
-   Key concepts, trade-offs, assumptions, and reasoning
-   Notes on reliability, scalability, latency, cost, evaluation, and
    safety
-   Other references and learning material relevant to the topics

The material is organized around the subject matter and the questions
being studied, rather than a daily checklist or progress log.

## Objective

The goal is to build a deeper understanding of **how AI systems work
beyond the model itself**: how requests flow through a system, how
components interact, how design choices affect cost and performance, and
how systems handle scale, failures, quality, and safety in production.

This is a learning repository, and its contents will evolve as I work
through the material.
